Analysis

A tuner and a spectrum for every input, and a mixer for every port on your interface.

Check pitch, frequency balance and levels while you play, without opening a second program.

Tuner

Chromatic, one per input, and it mutes the output while you tune.

Spectrum

Left and right bands in real time, so you see the low end before it muddies the mix.

Mixer

A fader, solo and mute for every input and output, adjustable while you play.

Tone Doctor

Points out fizz, mud and boom against a genre reference and applies the fix with one click.

DI loop

Loop a dry DI file through the chain and dial the tone with your hands off the guitar.
