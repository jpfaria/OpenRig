Practice

Practice with a drummer, a backing band and a looper without leaving the app.

75 drum grooves in 15 genres played by real drummers, 49 backing tracks you can slow down, and a metronome that follows the same tempo as the drums.

Drums

Pick a kit and a groove, hit FILL when the chorus comes, and set the tempo from the top bar.

Backing tracks

49 rock, blues and funk jams split into solo, rhythm, bass and acoustic, slowed to 0.5× without changing key or shifted ±12 semitones.

Metronome

Tap tempo, time signature, subdivision and count-in, sent to the output you choose.

Looper

Up to 8 loopers per chain and 8 layers each, with undo, reverse and half or double speed.
