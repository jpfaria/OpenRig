Instruments

Guitar, acoustic and vocals play at the same time, each through its own chain.

Every instrument gets its own inputs, gear and outputs, so a change on the vocal mic never touches the guitar.

  • Electric guitar

    Amps, cabs and the whole pedalboard.

  • Acoustic guitar

    Body models, mic chain and room.

  • Vocals

    Mic chain, compression and reverb.

  • Anything else

    A generic chain for whatever you plug in.
